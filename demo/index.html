bubble-pendant 头像旁的小对话气泡 · speech-bubble avatar pendant

每次点「重新进入」相当于重新打开聊天页：气泡「啵」地弹出来，从启用的组合里抽一组，这次打开期间不变；点一下气泡会晃一下。Each “Re-enter” simulates opening the chat again: the bubbles pop in, one preset is picked and kept. Tap a bubble to make it wiggle.
